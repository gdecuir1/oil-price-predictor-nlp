## Article Body

Oil and gas industry leaders are cautioning that the ongoing Iran-U.S. conflict could lead to substantial shifts in the global energy system. The war, which began in late February 2026, has seen significant escalation with the closure of the Strait of Hormuz, a critical chokepoint for global oil shipments. The blockade has resulted in a historical disruption of oil and gas supply, impacting approximately 20% of global oil and LNG shipments. Efforts to negotiate a resolution have stalled, with over 40 critical energy facilities reported damaged. The situation has caused widespread concern among energy market participants, as the conflict shows no signs of de-escalation.

## Market Interpretation

Market activity reflects a high-impact scenario consistent with a YES outcome, as the ongoing conflict and strategic blockades significantly disrupt oil supply chains. The probability of WTI Crude Oil prices reaching high levels in May 2026 appears to be supported by current market conditions. The impact is categorized as high, given the severity of supply disruptions and geopolitical instability.

## What to Watch

Observers should monitor key developments such as potential U.S.-Iran negotiations and any changes in the status of the Strait of Hormuz. Additionally, announcements from OPEC+ regarding production adjustments and reports from the EIA on crude inventory levels could influence market dynamics. The geopolitical landscape remains volatile, and any shifts could alter current pricing trends.
