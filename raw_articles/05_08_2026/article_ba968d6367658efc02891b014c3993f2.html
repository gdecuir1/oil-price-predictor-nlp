Global Markets Par Pressure, Indian Equities Thodi Dhyaan Se

Indian stocks aaj thoda halka start de sakte hain, kyuki GIFT Nifty around 24,294.50 par trade ho raha hai. Iski wajah hai global markets ka negative sentiment. US stocks bhi pichle din gir gaye the, khas kar chip stocks. U.S. aur Iran ke beech chalti uncertainty ne markets ko aur pressure mein dala hai, jisse S&P 500 0.38%, Nasdaq 0.13%, aur Dow Jones 0.63% neeche aaye.

Geopolitical Tensions Aur Assets Ka Khel

Middle East mein US aur Iran ke beech firing ki khabron ke baad crude oil prices mein izafa dikha. Dollar Index bhi strong hua hai kyunki Strait of Hormuz se energy flows ko lekar koi deal nahi hui hai. Gold prices stable hain kyuki Strait of Hormuz ke dobara khulne ki ummeed kam ho gayi hai aur inflation ki concerns phir se badh gayi hain. US bond yields thoda kam hue hain.

Fund Flows Ki Kahani: FIIs Bechte Hain, DIIs Kharidte Hain

Kal, May 7th ko, institutional investors ka picture mix tha. Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) ne ₹340 crore ke shares beche hain, jo ki is saal ke ₹2 lakh crore se zyada ke outflow trend ko continue karta hai. Dusri taraf, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) ne ₹441 crore ki khareedari karke support diya hai. DIIs is saal bahut active rahe hain, jinke inflows ₹3 lakh crore se paar ho gaye hain, jo foreign selling ko handle karne mein help kar rahe hain.

Market Ka Analysis

Nifty 50 index 24,326.65 par close hua tha, jiska Price-to-Earnings (P/E) ratio 21.2 hai. Yeh valuation abhi bhi historical norms mein hai. Middle East conflict aksar crude oil prices badhate hain, rupee ko kamzor karte hain, aur FIIs ko bechne par majboor karte hain. Lekin history dekhi jaye toh market kuch mahino mein recover ho jaati hai. Agar crude oil $100 ke upar raha, toh India ki inflation 30-40 basis points tak badh sakti hai aur current account deficit bhi khul jayega.

Risk Kya Hai?

FIIs ka lagatar bechna aur crude oil prices ka $100 barrel se upar jana India ki economy ke liye bada risk hai. Isse inflation badh sakti hai aur Reserve Bank of India (RBI) ko interest rates high rakhne pad sakte hain. Strong Dollar Index bhi Indian Rupee ko kamzor kar sakta hai.

Aage Kya?

Analysts keh rahe hain ki agar Nifty 24,200 ke upar sustain karta hai toh woh 25,000 tak ja sakta hai. Key support 24,228 par hai aur resistance 24,459 par. Geopolitical tensions aur rising oil prices se thoda volatility rahega, par DIIs ka support aur diplomatic efforts se market mein stability aa sakti hai.
