KKR swoops in to bolster HMC Capital’s green dreams in $600m deal

Sarah Thompson, Kanika Sood and Emma Rapaport
Feb 5, 2026 – 5.24pm
 or Subscribe to save article
EmailLinkedInTwitterFacebook

HMC Capital, the asset management empire founded and run by David Di Pilla, has found a white knight for its faltering energy transition play, luring one of the world’s largest private equity firms to back the strategy.

KKR is expected to make a “significant” investment into the fund, with some people briefed on the transaction suggesting it could reach $600 million. Those people requested anonymity for legal reasons, and Di Pilla declined to comment when contacted for comment late on Thursday.
