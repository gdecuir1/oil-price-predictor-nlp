About

Exports

In 2024, Kazakhstan exported $46.4B of Crude Petroleum, making it the 8th largest exporter of Crude Petroleum (out of 138) in the world. During the same year, Crude Petroleum were the most exported product (out of 1,134) in Kazakhstan. In 2024, the main destinations of Kazakhstan's Crude Petroleum exports were: Italy ($17.7B), Netherlands ($4.68B), France ($3.23B), Greece ($2.83B), and Austria ($2.72B).

The fastest growing markets for Crude Petroleum exports from Kazakhstan between 2023 and 2024 were: Italy ($13.6B), Bulgaria ($1.48B), and Netherlands ($1.33B).

Imports

In 2024, Kazakhstan imported $4.23k of Crude Petroleum, becoming the 120th largest importer of Crude Petroleum (out of 149) in the world. During the same year, Crude Petroleum were the 1,175th most imported product (out of 1,201) in Kazakhstan. In 2024, Kazakhstan imported Crude Petroleum primarily from: Netherlands ($2.22k), Kuwait ($1.91k), and United Arab Emirates ($100).

The fastest growing origins for Crude Petroleum imports in Kazakhstan between 2023 and 2024 was: Netherlands ($1.73k).

Product Classification

Crude Petroleum are part of Mineral Fuels, Oils, Waxes, and its Distillated Derivatives and include: Crude Petroleum Oils.
