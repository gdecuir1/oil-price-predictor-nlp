As the major oil companies pour capital into Namibia’s Orange Basin, small producer Oregen Energy is positioning itself for a significant farm-out deal. Mason Granger, CEO of Oregen Energy Corp. (CSE: ORNG | FSE: A1S0), explains why the company’s new acreage is not merely a land grab, but a strategic move years in the making together with local partner Petrovena.
